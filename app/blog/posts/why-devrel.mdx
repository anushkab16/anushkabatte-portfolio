I have spent the last two years building AI systems, writing research papers, 
and explaining both to people who had never heard of either.

The building part is interesting. The explaining part is what I love.

Developer Relations sits at that exact intersection — technically credible enough 
to earn trust, communicative enough to actually be useful. That's the job I want.

I'm Anushka. I'm 20, studying Computer Engineering in Mumbai, and I'm building 
this portfolio in public as I work toward a DevRel role at a company whose 
developer tools I actually use.

This blog is where I think out loud. About AI, about developer experience, 
about what it means to make complex things make sense.
